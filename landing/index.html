// The Problem

Fee collection shouldn't be a spreadsheet marathon.

For most coaching centers, fee collection is still a manual, repetitive grind. Administrators juggle spreadsheets, sticky notes, and individual parent messages — losing hours every month that should go to teaching.

Scattered spreadsheets

Student fee data lives across multiple sheets and devices, with no single source of truth.

Manual follow-ups

Every overdue fee means a hand-typed message. Hours lost rewriting the same reminder.

No risk visibility

No way to tell which student will actually pay on time and who needs a gentle nudge.

No status tracking

Did the parent reply? Did they pay? Who knows — the spreadsheet wasn't updated.

// Features

Everything an administrator needs to collect fees — with AI doing the heavy lifting.

AI Payment-Risk Analysis

Google Gemini evaluates each student's payment history and outputs a 0–100 risk score, a predicted payment date, and a personalized Hinglish reminder — in one call.

  • › Risk score 0–100
  • › Predicted payment date
  • › Empathetic, non-threatening tone

Collection Dashboard

A single screen for total students, pending payments, messages sent, parent responses, paid count, total due, collected amount, collection rate, and high-risk students.

  • › Real-time KPI overview
  • › Recharts visualizations
  • › Risk-stratified student view

Student Management

Track every student's name, parent, phone, course, monthly fee, outstanding amount, due date, notes, payment status, AI risk score, predicted date, and generated message.

pending message_sent replied paid

Bulk CSV Upload

Skip the manual entry. Prepare students in CSV format, drop the file in, and import dozens of records at once. A sample CSV ships with the repo for testing.

  • › Drag-and-drop upload
  • › Backend CSV format validation
  • › Instant AI follow-up analysis

WhatsApp-Ready Reminders

Each generated message is designed to be copy-pasted into WhatsApp. Natural Hinglish tone, parent's name, student's context — respectful, warm, and useful.

"Not just an automated reminder — communication that feels personal."

Auth & Institution Isolation

Firebase Authentication handles sign-up, login, email verification, and logout. Each institution operates in its own isolated context — student data stays scoped to the right center.

  • › Email/password + verification
  • › Per-institution data scoping
  • › Firebase Admin SDK on backend
// How It Works

The user flow, end to end.

Follow the dots. From sign-up to a fully updated dashboard — every step in the DueAlert workflow, with Gemini AI doing the analysis in the middle. Watch the pulse travel through each node.

Admin workflow steps AI-powered steps (Gemini) Live data pulse
1. Institution creates an account
2. Email verification via Firebase

// AI vs Manual

From manual grind to AI-assisted intelligence.

Traditional Workflow

  1. 01 Open spreadsheet, scan for overdue fees
  2. 02 Manually identify who needs follow-up
  3. 03 Decide who's likely to delay — by gut feel
  4. 04 Write each reminder by hand
  5. 05 Send messages one by one
  6. 06 Update records manually (if at all)
⏱ Hours per week lost on repetitive manual work

DueAlert Workflow

  1. 01 DueAlert loads student payment data
  2. 02 Gemini evaluates payment risk per student
  3. 03 Predicted payment date generated
  4. 04 Risk score 0–100 assigned
  5. 05 Personalized Hinglish message drafted
  6. 06 Admin reviews & sends via WhatsApp
  7. 07 Status auto-tracked on dashboard
⚡ AI handles analysis & drafting — admin handles judgment
// Use Cases

Built for any institution that collects recurring fees.

Any organization that tracks students, monthly fees, and parent communication can plug into DueAlert's workflow.

📚

Coaching & Tuition Centers

Track monthly tuition fees across batches, identify parents who consistently delay, and send warm reminders in Hinglish — without the spreadsheet dance.

🏫

Private Schools & Academies

Bulk-import entire classes via CSV, let Gemini flag high-risk accounts early in the cycle, and centralize fee status visibility for the admin office.

🎓

Test Prep Institutes

JEE, NEET, UPSC, CAT — multi-batch institutes with quarterly installments can predict payment dates and prioritize follow-ups by AI risk score.

⚽

Sports Academies

Cricket, football, swimming academies with monthly coaching dues can manage parent communication and payment tracking from one dashboard.

🎵

Music & Dance Schools

Small studios with 20–100 students often rely on WhatsApp memory. DueAlert gives them a proper system with AI-drafted reminders ready to send.

💻

Online Course Creators

For cohort-based courses with EMI payment plans, DueAlert can flag at-risk installments and personalize follow-ups based on each learner's payment history.
